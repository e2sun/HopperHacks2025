# 🐺 HopperHacks 2025 – Seawolf Connect

## 💡 Inspiration

Seawolf Connect was born from the desire to help students dive deeper into Stony Brook’s vibrant campus life. With over 25,000 students, finding peers with shared interests can be overwhelming. 

As an incoming freshman, I was lucky to discover my WISE (Women in Science and Engineering) community—a supportive network where I met some of my closest friends. Now, with **Seawolf Connect**, I hope to recreate that welcoming space for every Seawolf—whether you’re looking to network, build friendships, or find the perfect study group.

---

## ⚙️ What It Does

Seawolf Connect features an interactive quiz that reveals your unique **"wolfienality"**—a fun mix of your personality and Seawolf spirit 🐺✨.

Once you receive your result, you can opt to add your information to our system. From there, you’re directed to a dedicated page where you can explore and connect with fellow Seawolves who share your mascot match.

---

## 🛠️ Built With Passion

- 💻 HTML, CSS, JavaScript  
- 🎨 Adobe Fresco (for custom Wolfie illustrations)  
- 🧩 Canva (for layout and result pages)  

We focused on building a modern, friendly, and interactive experience for users from all backgrounds.

---

## 🧱 Challenges We Ran Into

- 🖼 **Image Integration**: Making sure our custom Wolfie illustrations were high-quality *and* responsive was tricky across different devices and layouts.
- 🔌 **Database Connectivity**: Designing a reliable system to store and retrieve users based on wolfienality required careful planning and testing.
- 🎨 **Styling and Layout**: Balancing visual appeal with readability was a challenge, especially when styling background images and mobile responsiveness.
